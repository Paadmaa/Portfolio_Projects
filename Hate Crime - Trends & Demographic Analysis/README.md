# 🧠 Hate Crime in the USA: A Power BI Storyboard  
**Uncovering patterns, amplifying voices, and driving awareness through data**

---

## 🎬 Why This Project Matters  
Hate crimes are not just statistics—they are stories of trauma, injustice, and resilience. This Power BI dashboard transforms raw data into a compelling narrative that highlights victimization patterns, offender demographics, and systemic gaps across the United States.

By visualizing these trends, the project aims to support advocacy, policy-making, and public awareness.

---

## 📦 Data Sources & Scope  
- **Victim & Offender Data**: State-wise counts, agency types, race, ethnicity  
- **Temporal Trends**: Year-by-year analysis from 1990 to 2020  
- **Demographics**: Breakdown by race, ethnicity, and reporting agency  
- **Geographic Distribution**: Heatmaps and bar charts by state  

---

## 📊 Dashboard Modules  

### 1️⃣ Victimization Analysis  
- Victim count by **state**, **agency type**, and **year**  
- Highlights states with highest victim counts (e.g., California, New York)  
- Adult vs. juvenile victim segmentation  

### 2️⃣ Offender Profiling  
- Offender count by **state**, **race**, and **ethnicity**  
- Agency-wise breakdown (City, County, Federal, Tribal)  
- Comparative analysis of offender vs. victim counts  

### 3️⃣ Temporal Trends  
- Year-over-year trends from 1990 to 2020  
- Spike detection and anomaly spotting  
- Correlation between social events and crime surges  

### 4️⃣ Advocacy & Action  
- What victims and bystanders can do  
- Famous personalities who were bullied  
- Educational callouts and social justice messaging  

---

## 🧠 Analytical Techniques  
- **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**: Outlier detection, distribution analysis  
- **Power BI Visuals**:  
  - Clustered bar charts  
  - Line graphs for temporal trends  
  - Heatmaps for geographic intensity  
- **Insight Cards**: Embedded social messages and calls to action  

---

## 💡 Key Insights  
- Over **200K offenders** and **260K victims** reported across 30 years  
- **Race/Ethnicity** accounts for 57% of hate crimes  
- **California** consistently ranks highest in both victim and offender counts  
- Significant underreporting in tribal and federal agencies  
- Hate crimes against Asian Americans surged in 2020
