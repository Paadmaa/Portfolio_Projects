# 🚴‍♂️ Bike Sales Performance Dashboard – Sales Report Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview  
This dashboard analyzes **bike and accessory sales performance** across customers, cities, and product categories. It compares actual sales against budget targets, identifies top-performing products and customers, and uncovers seasonal trends across months and years.

The goal: Deliver **clear visibility into revenue drivers, budget alignment, and customer behavior** to support strategic sales planning.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Track **monthly sales vs budget** to monitor performance gaps.
- Identify **top-selling products and customers** for targeted engagement.
- Analyze **city-wise sales distribution** to optimize regional strategies.
- Understand **seasonal patterns** in bike sales across years.
- Segment performance by **product category and sub-category**.
