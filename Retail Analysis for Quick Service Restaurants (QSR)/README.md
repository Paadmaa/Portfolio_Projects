# 🍔 Crunchy Corner Business Optimization & Budgeting  
**Analyzing financial performance, operational efficiency, and strategic budgeting for India’s largest fast food chain**

---

## 📘 Project Overview  
This Power BI dashboard analyzes the financial and operational performance of **Crunchy Corner**, a fast food chain with over 1,000 outlets across India. The goal is to optimize business operations, identify high-performing categories, and support strategic budgeting decisions.

The dashboard provides insights into sales, profit margins, SKU contributions, and cost drivers—empowering stakeholders to make data-driven decisions.

---

## 📂 Dataset Summary  
The dataset includes:

| Category             | Description                                      |
|----------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Financial Metrics    | Sales, Gross Profit, EBITDA, PAT                 |
| SKU Data             | Product-level sales and profitability            |
| Channel Performance  | Sales by delivery, dine-in, and takeaway         |
| Budgeting Metrics    | Actual vs. Budget comparisons for cost and revenue |
| Time Dimensions      | Monthly, Year-to-Date (YTD), and historical trends |

---

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights  

### 🔢 Financial KPIs  
- **Total Sales**, **Gross Profit**, **EBITDA**, **PAT**  
- **YoY Trends** for all financial metrics  
- **100% Stake Column Chart** showing proportional breakdown  
- **Sales by Category & Location**  
- **Channel-wise Sales Bifurcation**  

### 📈 Optimization Insights  
- **Top Categories by Gross Profit & Net Revenue** (Scatter Plot)  
- **Pareto Analysis**: Identifies top 20% SKUs contributing 80% of revenue  
- **Mekko Chart**: SKU contribution by category  
- **Quadrant Analysis**: High-performing categories and locations  
- **Gross Profit vs. Volume Comparison**  

### 📊 Budgeting & Planning  
- **PVM Analysis**: Price, Volume, Mix impact on revenue  
- **Actual vs. Budget**: Financial and cost drivers (COGS, Packaging, Marketing)  
- **Variance Analysis**: Highlights deviations and trends  

---

## 🧠 Analytical Techniques  
- **Advanced DAX Measures**:  
  - Running totals, cumulative percentages, conditional formatting  
  - MAT (Moving Annual Total) comparisons  
  - Profit contribution and SKU ranking  
- **Dynamic Filtering**: Timeframe selection (Monthly vs. YTD)  
- **Custom Visuals**: Mekko chart, quadrant plots, Pareto layers

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations  
- Focus on **top-performing SKUs** for revenue growth  
- Optimize **low-margin categories** through cost control  
- Use **Pareto insights** to streamline inventory and marketing  
- Align budgeting with **actual performance trends**  
- Target **high-contribution locations** for expansion
