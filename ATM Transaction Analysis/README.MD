# 🏦 Wisabi Bank ATM Transactions Analysis  
**Enhancing ATM network efficiency and customer experience through data-driven insights**

---

## 📘 Project Overview  
This project presents a comprehensive analysis of ATM transaction data from **Wisabi Bank**, covering over **2 million transactions** across **five Nigerian states** throughout **2022**. The objective is to uncover patterns in ATM usage, transaction types, and customer demographics to inform strategic decisions around ATM placement, operational efficiency, and service optimization.

Leveraging **Power BI** for visualization and **SQL** for data transformation, the dashboard delivers actionable insights that support both tactical improvements and long-term planning.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives  
- Identify high-performing and underutilized ATMs across regions  
- Analyze transaction types and durations to optimize service delivery  
- Understand customer behavior by age group, occupation, and location  
- Recommend strategies to improve ATM availability and reduce congestion

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features  
- **State-Level Utilization Analysis**: Compare ATM performance across Kano, Lagos, Rivers, FCT, and Kaduna  
- **Transaction Type Breakdown**: Visualize frequency, amount, and duration by transaction category  
- **Demographic Insights**: Explore usage patterns by age group and occupation  
- **Temporal Trends**: Monitor daily and monthly transaction volumes and peak hours  
- **Performance Metrics**: Highlight average transaction duration and total transaction value

## ✅ Key Insights & Impact  
- **Kano** recorded the highest ATM utilization and longest transaction durations  
- **FCT** showed significantly lower usage, indicating potential for ATM redistribution  
- **Balance inquiries and transfers** contributed to ATM congestion—suggesting a shift to digital channels  
- **Occupation and age group analysis** revealed distinct usage patterns, enabling targeted service enhancements
