## 📘 Project Overview  
This Power BI dashboard analyzes credit card transactions, customer demographics, and revenue streams across card categories, expenditure types, and income groups. It helps financial institutions understand customer value, optimize card offerings, and target high-revenue segments.

The dashboard supports strategic decisions in marketing, product design, and customer retention.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives  
- Analyze revenue and transaction volume by card category  
- Identify top-performing customer segments by job, income, and education  
- Track revenue trends across quarters and states  
- Understand expenditure behavior and channel preferences (swipe, chip, online)  
- Segment customers by age, gender, marital status, and dependents

## ✅ Key Insights & Impact  
- **Blue card holders** generate the highest revenue and interest  
- **Businessmen and white-collar professionals** are top contributors to income and revenue  
- **Swipe transactions** dominate usage, followed by chip and online  
- **Age group 40–50** and **high-income customers** are the most profitable segments  
- **Texas and New York** lead in state-level revenue performance  
- **Graduate education level** correlates with highest revenue generation
