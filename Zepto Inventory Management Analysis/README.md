# 🛒 **ZeptoPulse: Sales & Operations Intelligence Dashboard**

## 📌 Project Overview  
This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive view of **Zepto’s sales performance, inventory health, delivery efficiency, and customer behavior**. It empowers business stakeholders to monitor KPIs, identify operational bottlenecks, and optimize product and regional strategies.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Track **total sales**, **average order value (AOV)**, and **return rate**.
- Identify **top-performing brands and products**.
- Monitor **inventory value and stock levels** by category.
- Analyze **delivery performance** and refund trends.
- Understand **return reasons** and order distribution by store and location.

## 📢 Insights Summary
- **High return rate (10%)** suggests need for quality control or better packaging.
- **Delivery delays (80%)** indicate logistics inefficiencies.
- **Farm Fresh and Amul** dominate brand sales — ideal for promotions.
- **Personal Care and Grocery** hold highest inventory value — monitor turnover.
- **Rajarhat and Kothrud** are top-performing locations — potential for expansion.
