# 📱 Mobile Sales Analytics 


This project dissects **769M+ in total sales** data from a mobile retail ecosystem, breaking down performance across **brands, models, cities, payment methods, and time periods**.  

Using rich visualizations and KPIs, it uncovers insights for **sales growth, operational optimization, and customer experience improvement**.

---

## 🎯 **Core Business Questions**
1. Which **brands and models** generate the highest revenue and transactions?  
2. How do **sales patterns vary** by city, month, and quarter?  
3. Which **payment methods** dominate customer preference?  
4. How does **current performance compare** to the same period last year?  
5. What **customer rating trends** reveal about product satisfaction?

---

## 📅 **Trend Highlights**
- **Quarterly Comparison:** Clear year-over-year growth in multiple quarters, with strategic spikes in Q1 and Q4.  
- **Monthly Seasonality:** Jan–March shows sustained volume, while festive season peaks impact Q4.  
- **Payment Mix:** Fairly even split between UPI, debit card, credit card, and cash — each around ~24–26%.

## 🛠 **Tech Stack**
- **Visualization:** Power BI  
- **Data Transformation:** Power Query / DAX  
- **Source:** Sales transactional and customer feedback dataset  
- **Version Control:** Git & GitHub for project tracking

---

## 📌 **Dashboard Features**
- **Dynamic filters** for brand, model, city, month, payment method  
- **Year-over-year comparisons** with variance indicators  
- **Performance ranking** by sales, quantity, transactions  
- **Customer satisfaction module** based on ratings

## 🎯 **Key Insights**
- **Apple, Samsung, and OnePlus** lead sales but face close competition — retention and upselling crucial.  
- **Tier-2 cities** (e.g., Ludhiana, Jodhpur) deliver surprising volumes — potential marketing goldmines.  
- **Balanced payment mix** reduces dependency on a single transaction mode.  
- **Slight dip in some quarters** indicates areas for tactical promotions.
