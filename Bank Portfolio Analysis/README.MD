# 💰 Bank Loan Analysis  
**Driving smarter lending decisions through data-driven insights**

---

## 📘 Project Overview  
This project presents a detailed analysis of loan application data from a financial institution, covering over **38,000 loan records** and more than **$435 million in funded amounts**. The goal is to evaluate loan performance, identify risk factors, and uncover trends in borrower behavior to support more informed lending strategies.

Using **Power BI** and **SQL**, the dashboard provides a comprehensive view of loan status, funding trends, borrower demographics, and repayment outcomes.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives  
- Assess the distribution and performance of good vs. bad loans  
- Analyze borrower characteristics such as employment length, home ownership, and loan purpose  
- Monitor monthly trends in funding and repayment  
- Identify high-risk segments and recommend mitigation strategies

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features  
- **Loan Status Breakdown**: Fully Paid, Charged Off, and Current loans  
- **Monthly Trends**: Funded amount and repayment volume over time  
- **Borrower Profile Analysis**: Employment length, home ownership, and loan purpose  
- **Term Comparison**: 36-month vs. 60-month loan performance  
- **Good vs. Bad Loan Segmentation**: Funding and repayment metrics

## ✅ Key Insights & Impact  
- **86.2% of loans** were classified as “Good,” accounting for **$370M in funding**  
- **Bad loans** represented **13.8%** of funded volume, with significantly lower repayment rates  
- Borrowers with **10+ years of employment** contributed the highest repayment volume ($126M)  
- **Debt consolidation** was the most common loan purpose, followed by credit card and home improvement  
- Loans with **60-month terms** had higher total repayment but also increased risk exposure
