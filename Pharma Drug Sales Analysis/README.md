# 💊 Pharma Drug Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 🖋 Project Overview  
This project dissects multi‑year pharmaceutical sales data to uncover **seasonal patterns, category performance, and sales drivers**.  
It blends **KPIs, trend charts, and granular category breakdowns** into a unified dashboard that can inform product, inventory, and marketing decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives  
1. Identify **top‑performing drug categories** by sales and quantity.  
2. Compare sales trends **weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly**.  
3. Analyze **hourly sales behavior** for demand forecasting.  
4. Visualize **sales contribution by category** to allocate resources efficiently.  
5. Benchmark **total vs. average monthly sales** over time.

## 📅 Trend Highlights
- **Yearly Sales (2014–2019):** Stable growth from $8K to $13K annually.  
- **Quarterly Performance:** Peaks near $20K in multiple quarters; consistent $30K–$34K in total monthly sales trend.  
- **Monthly Seasonality:** Total monthly sales ranging $9K–$13K; average monthly sales hover around $10K–$11K.  
- **Weekly Sales by Category:** Leading category hits just under $4K/week.  
- **Hourly Sales Patterns:** Sales spike at certain hours — up to 1K units/hour in some categories.
