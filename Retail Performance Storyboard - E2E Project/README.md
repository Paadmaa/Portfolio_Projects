# 📊 Retail Analytics Excellence — From Store Floors to Strategic Boardrooms

## 1. Executive Overview
This unified analytics suite blends **two complementary perspectives**:
- **Reliance SMART Dashboard** → Store‑level, brand‑level, and geographic sales intelligence.
- **Corporate Sales & Profit Dashboard** → Enterprise‑wide performance with product, channel, and customer profitability deep dives.

Together, they deliver an **end‑to‑end retail intelligence framework**:  
From **transaction counts in Washington state** to **margin by export channel**, decision‑makers can see the *full value chain* in one analytic experience.

1. How are we performing against monthly revenue and profit targets?
