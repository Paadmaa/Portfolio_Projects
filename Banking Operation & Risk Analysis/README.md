# 🧾 Banking Operations & Risk Analysis  
**Leveraging data to minimize lending risk and optimize client engagement**

---

## 📘 Project Overview  
This project explores how banks can use data analytics to assess client risk, improve lending decisions, and monitor key financial metrics. Using **Power BI**, I developed a multi-layered dashboard that visualizes loan exposure, deposit behavior, client engagement, and fee structures across various banking relationships.

The analysis is powered by a relational dataset containing client profiles, banking products, advisor interactions, and financial timelines — enabling a holistic view of banking operations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives  
- Evaluate client risk profiles to support smarter lending decisions  
- Monitor total loan exposure across banking products and demographics  
- Analyze deposit behavior and account types to guide investment strategy  
- Track engagement duration and fee structures to assess client value

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features  
- **Loan Analysis**: Total loan breakdown by gender, advisor, and product type  
- **Deposit Analysis**: Savings, checking, foreign currency, and bank deposits  
- **Client Engagement**: Time spent with bank, income bands, and fee impact  
- **Summary Dashboard**: KPI cards for total clients, deposits, loans, and fees  
- **Calculated Metrics**: SUMX, DISTINCTCOUNT, SWITCH, DATEDIFF for dynamic insights

## ✅ Key Insights & Impact  
- **Private banks** have the highest client volume, suggesting stronger engagement strategies  
- Clients with **higher income bands** show longer engagement and larger deposits  
- **Processing fees** vary based on fee structure and loan amount, impacting total revenue  
- **Engagement timeframe** helps identify loyal clients and retention opportunities  
- **Nationality-based loan exposure** offers strategic insights for regional targeting
