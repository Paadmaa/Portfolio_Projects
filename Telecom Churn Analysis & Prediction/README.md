# 📞 Telecom Churn Analysis & Prediction  
**Reducing customer churn through data-driven insights and interactive dashboards**

---

## 📘 Project Overview  
This project focuses on understanding and mitigating customer churn in the telecom industry. Using **Power BI**, **EDA**, and business storytelling, the goal is to help telecom providers identify churn risks, visualize customer behavior, and implement retention strategies.

The dashboard and analysis are designed to support strategic decisions in marketing, customer service, and product development.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives  
- Identify key drivers of customer churn  
- Segment customers based on churn risk and behavior  
- Visualize churn scenarios and subscriber decision cycles  
- Provide actionable insights to improve retention and reduce revenue loss
