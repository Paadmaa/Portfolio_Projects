## 🧭 Project Purpose  
This Power BI dashboard analyzes 19 months of emergency room data to uncover patterns in patient demographics, wait times, admission rates, and departmental referrals. The goal is to support hospital administrators in improving operational efficiency, staffing decisions, and patient experience.

---

## 📦 Dataset Overview  
| Metric                     | Description                              |
|----------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Patient Volume             | 9,216 unique patients (Apr 2023–Oct 2024) |
| Wait Time & Satisfaction   | Avg wait time: 35.3 min; Satisfaction: 4.99/10 |
| Admission Status           | 50.04% Admitted, 49.96% Not-Admitted     |
| Demographics               | Age, Gender, Race, Referral Department   |
| Temporal Dimensions        | Day of week, hour of day                 |

---

## 📊 Dashboard Modules  

### 🧍 Patient Demographics  
- Age group distribution (peak: 30–39 years)  
- Gender split: ~51% Female, ~49% Male  
- Race breakdown: White, African American, Asian, Multi-racial  

### 🕒 Wait Time & Satisfaction  
- Avg wait time: 35.3 minutes  
- Satisfaction score: 4.99/10  
- % of patients seen within 30 minutes  

### 🏥 Admission & Referral Analysis  
- Admission vs. non-admission rates  
- Referral departments: General Practice, Orthopedics, Cardiology  
- 5.4K patients had no referral  

### 📅 Temporal Load Analysis  
- Busiest days: Monday, Saturday, Tuesday  
- Peak hours: 11 AM, 1 PM, 7 PM, 11 PM  
- Hourly breakdown by day of week  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights  
- Nearly equal split between admitted and non-admitted patients  
- High traffic during late night and early morning hours  
- General Practice and Orthopedics dominate referrals  
- Moderate satisfaction scores suggest room for service improvement  
- Diverse racial representation with notable non-disclosure rates  

---

## 🧠 Analytical Techniques  
- Descriptive statistics and trend analysis  
- Time-series breakdown by hour and weekday  
- Conditional formatting for performance flags  
- Patient-level drill-down for granular insights
