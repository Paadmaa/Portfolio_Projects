# 🚗 Car Sales Performance Dashboard  
**Analyzing revenue, customer demographics, and dealer performance across cities and models**

---

## 📘 Project Overview  
This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of car sales data across multiple cities, companies, and dealers. It visualizes key metrics such as total revenue, cars sold, model distribution, and customer income — helping stakeholders identify top-performing dealers, profitable car styles, and demographic trends.

The dashboard is interactive, allowing users to filter by year and city to uncover localized insights and revenue patterns.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives  
- Track total revenue and sales volume across dealers and car models  
- Identify high-performing companies and body styles by revenue  
- Analyze customer demographics (gender, income) to guide marketing strategy  
- Evaluate dealer performance and pricing trends for strategic planning

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features  
- **Key Metrics**:  
  - 24K Cars Sold  
  - $672M Total Revenue  
  - 154 Models, 30 Companies, 28 Dealers  

- **Demographic Analysis**:  
  - Annual income by gender (Male: $16B, Female: $4B)  
  - Body style preferences segmented by gender  

- **Revenue Breakdown**:  
  - Revenue by body style (SUV, Hatchback, Sedan, etc.)  
  - Revenue trends over time (Jan 2022 to Oct 2023)  
  - Top 5 companies by revenue (Chevrolet, Ford, Dodge, etc.)  
  - Dealer-level revenue and entry counts  

- **Model-Level Insights**:  
  - Scatter plot showing average price vs. revenue by car model  
  - Identifies high-revenue models and pricing efficiency

## ✅ Key Insights & Impact  
- **SUVs and Hatchbacks** dominate revenue share, accounting for over 50% of total sales  
- **Chevrolet and Ford** lead in company-level revenue, indicating strong market presence  
- **Male customers** contribute over 80% of total income, suggesting targeted outreach potential  
- **Rabun Used Car Sales** and **Progressive Shippers Cooperative** are top dealers by revenue  
- **Revenue trends** show seasonal spikes, useful for inventory and campaign planning
