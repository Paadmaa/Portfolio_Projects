# Cryptocurrency ₿ Data Analysis & Forecasting  
**Tracking price trends, volume shifts, and performance across top cryptocurrencies**

---

## 📘 Project Overview  
This project analyzes historical price and volume data for major cryptocurrencies using Power BI. It provides insights into market capitalization, price volatility, and comparative performance across coins like Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, and Dogecoin. The dashboard supports investors, analysts, and fintech teams in understanding crypto trends and making informed decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives  
- Track historical price movements and volume trends  
- Compare performance across multiple cryptocurrencies  
- Identify best and worst performers over 30-day and 365-day periods  
- Visualize price correlations and moving averages  
- Forecast future price trends using Power BI and Python (optional extension)

## 📁 Dataset Overview  
- **Source**: Daily price history from April 28, 2013  
- **Format**: One CSV file per cryptocurrency  
- **Fields**: Date, Open, High, Low, Close, Volume, Market Cap  

### 🪙 Coins Analyzed  
Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, Dogecoin, Binance Coin, Cardano, Polkadot, Chainlink, XRP, Litecoin, Tether, USD Coin, Stellar, Uniswap, Cosmos, IOTA, EOS, NEM, Monacoin, Wrapped Bitcoin, Crypto.com Coin

## ✅ Key Insights & Impact  
- **DOGE and SOL** showed strong growth over both short and long terms  
- **EOS and DOT** underperformed in recent months  
- **Volume spikes** aligned with major market events  
- **Price correlations** reveal strong relationships between BTC, ETH, and BNB  
- **30-day moving averages** help smooth volatility and guide trend analysis
