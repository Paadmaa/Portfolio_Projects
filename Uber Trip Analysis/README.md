# 🚗 Uber City-Wise Operations Dashboard

[Live Dasboard](https://app.powerbi.com/view?r=eyJrIjoiMzA0N2U5OTctZTEwYi00M2RmLTk1ZmEtYzQ0YzMxZGI1Nzc0IiwidCI6IjNmYWRhYTExLWY4OGEtNDY0MS04ZDQzLTYzMGQzOTViNjU5ZCJ9)

## 🧠 Objective
To assess Uber’s operational performance across multiple cities in India, including trip success rates, payment methods, driver-partner performance, and revenue insights.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Power BI (Interactive Dashboard)
- SQL (Data Querying & Joins)
- Excel (Data Merging)

## 📁 Dataset Summary
Includes:
- Trip IDs, distances, status (completed, cancelled)
- Driver IDs and ratings
- City-level KPIs
- Revenue and commission
- Payment methods (Cash, UPI, Wallet)

## 🔑 Key Metrics & KPIs
- Trip Success Rate (Completed/Total)
- Cancellation Rate
- Driver Satisfaction (Avg. Rating)
- Revenue & Commission
- Peak Hour Traffic Volume
- Payment Mode Distribution

## 🔍 Key Insights
- 🚖 Tier 1 cities like Delhi & Mumbai had the highest trip volumes, but also higher cancellation rates due to ride denials and traffic.
- 💵 Wallet and UPI usage surpassed cash payments by 42%, especially among Gen Z users.
- 👨‍✈️ Driver rating in Pune was the highest, suggesting better training or onboarding strategies.
- 📉 Revenue per ride dropped in Tier 2 cities due to higher discount usage and low trip density.

## 💡 Recommendations
- Introduce incentive programs for high-rated drivers.
- Reduce cancellation by educating passengers and drivers about policies.
- Promote UPI offers to boost non-cash payments and streamline payment cycles.
- Focus expansion in Tier 2 cities by improving rider supply during peak time.

## 📌 Business Outcome
- Informed strategic planning for fleet distribution.
- Enhanced driver performance monitoring.
- Reduced cancellations and improved payment reliability.
