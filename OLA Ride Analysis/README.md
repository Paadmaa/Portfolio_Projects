# 🚖 OLA July 2024 Performance Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview  
This project analyzes OLA’s **ride-booking, revenue, and operational performance** for **July 2024**.  
It distills millions in booking value into **clear KPIs, visual dashboards, and optimization opportunities** across booking trends, payment preferences, and vehicle-type utilization.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives
1. Track **total ride bookings** and **overall booking value**.  
2. Understand **cancellation dynamics** and reduce failed transactions.  
3. Identify **peak and low-demand days** to optimize driver allocation.  
4. Break down **revenue by payment method** for better cashflow management.  
5. Compare **vehicle-type contributions** to both booking value and operational efficiency.

---

## 📅 Trend Analysis
- **Ride Volume:** Fluctuates between ~3,000–3,400 daily rides.  
- **Revenue by Date:** Steady range of ₹30K–₹50K/day.  
- **Cancellation Impact:** ~28% of bookings did not convert, pointing to operational improvement opportunities.

## 📢 Key Takeaways
- **Cash & UPI** dominate revenue inflow — potential to push digital payments further.  
- **Prime Sedan, Prime Plus, E-Bike** lead in booking value and distance efficiency.  
- **Cancellations by drivers** form the largest lost-opportunity segment.  
- **Auto rides** have lowest avg. trip distance — candidate for short-route dynamic pricing.

## 🚀 Next Steps
- Launch **driver incentive program** for ride completion.  
- Develop **real-time driver-customer matching** to cut “driver not found” incidents.  
- Introduce **UPI cashback offers** to accelerate digital adoption.  
- Leverage **vehicle-type distance efficiency** for marketing route-specific services.
